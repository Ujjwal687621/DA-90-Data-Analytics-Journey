## Company Overview

The company provides data-driven products and services across payments, personal finance, merchant analytics, fraud detection, and customer success.

The fictional business environment provides a consistent context for analyzing realistic business problems and demonstrating practical data analytics skills.

## Analyst Role

The portfolio simulates the responsibilities of a Junior Data Analyst supporting business teams with data-driven analysis and reporting.

Typical responsibilities include:

- Writing SQL queries to answer business questions
- Cleaning and preparing datasets
- Analyzing business performance and trends
- Developing Excel-based analysis and reports
- Building Power BI dashboards
- Developing KPIs and analytical calculations
- Investigating business performance drivers
- Identifying data limitations and additional data requirements
- Communicating findings to business stakeholders
- Translating analytical results into actionable business questions and recommendations

## Analytical Approach

Projects follow a practical end-to-end analytics workflow:

**Business Problem → Data → Cleaning → Analysis → Modeling → Visualization → Insights → Business Recommendations**

The focus is on connecting technical analysis with business context rather than producing analysis in isolation.
