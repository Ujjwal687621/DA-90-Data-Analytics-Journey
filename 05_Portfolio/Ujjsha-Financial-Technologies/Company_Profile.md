# 🏢 Ujjsha Financial Technologies

Every number tells a story. Every insight drives a decision.

## Overview

Ujjsha Financial Technologies is a fictional fintech company created as part of **Project DA-90**.

This portfolio simulates the work of a Data Analyst supporting different business teams through SQL, Excel, Tableau, Python, and business intelligence projects.

Rather than completing isolated tutorials, every project in this portfolio contributes to the same fictional company, creating a realistic end-to-end analytics experience.
