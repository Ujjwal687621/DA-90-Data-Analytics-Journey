## Project Overview

Ujjsha Retail is a fictional retail business created for Portfolio Project #1 within the DA-90 Data Analytics Journey.

The objective of this project was to analyze sales performance, identify the primary drivers of revenue changes, and build an interactive Power BI report that communicates actionable findings to business stakeholders.

The project follows an end-to-end analytical workflow:

**Data → Cleaning → Analysis → Modeling → DAX → Visualization → Business Findings → Management Investigation**

---

## Business Problem

Management wanted to understand overall sales performance and identify where revenue changes were occurring.

The analysis focused on questions such as:

- How is overall revenue performing?
- Which categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products contribute most to revenue?
- Where are the largest changes occurring?
- What products and categories are driving the August revenue decline?
- What additional information is required to determine the underlying causes?
- What should management investigate next?

# Key Business Findings

## Overall Performance

The analysis covers:

- **$224.87K** in total revenue
- **200** transactions
- **742** units sold
- **$303.06** average selling price
- **3.71** average items per transaction

---

## Revenue Trend

Monthly revenue was:

| Month  | Revenue |
| ------ | ------: |
| May    | $40.33K |
| June   | $84.75K |
| July   | $57.24K |
| August | $42.55K |

Revenue peaked in June before declining in July and August.

August revenue declined by:

**$14.69K (-25.66%) compared with July.**

---

## Product Concentration

The Top 5 products generated:

**$116.08K — 51.62% of total revenue**

The highest-revenue products were:

1. Mesh Wifi System — $34,456.73
2. Ethernet Adapter — $24,833.94
3. Wi-Fi Extender — $20,782.00
4. Desktop Computer — $18,036.72
5. Workstation — $17,970.25

Computer and Networking generated:

**68.98% of total revenue.**

---

## August Revenue Decline

Computer and Networking accounted for:

**90.44% of the net August revenue decline.**

The largest product-level declines included:

- Mesh Wi-Fi System — approximately -$8.5K
- Desktop Computer — approximately -$6.9K
- Wi-Fi Router — approximately -$1.6K

The Office category partially offset declines elsewhere by increasing revenue by approximately $2.3K.

---

# Executive Insight

> Revenue peaked at $84.75K in June after increasing 110.16% from May, then declined to $57.24K in July and $42.55K in August. The trend indicates strong mid-period growth followed by moderation in the subsequent two months.

---

# Management Investigation

The analysis identifies where revenue declined but does not establish a definitive cause.

Management should investigate:

- Inventory availability
- Stockouts
- Pricing changes
- Promotions and discounts
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Product mix
- Competitive pricing
- Product demand

Particular attention should be given to the Mesh Wi-Fi System and Desktop Computer because they were major contributors to the observed decline.

---

# Recommended Next Step

For the Mesh Wi-Fi System, management should conduct a competitive pricing analysis.

If Ujjsha's price is materially higher than comparable competitors, a temporary targeted price reduction or promotional offer could be evaluated as a controlled experiment.

The experiment should measure:

**Competitor Price → Pricing Decision → Units Sold → Transaction Volume → Revenue → Gross Profit / Margin**

Any pricing decision should consider product margin so that increased sales volume does not come at an unacceptable profitability cost.

---

# Data Limitations

The dataset supports performance analysis and identification of potential investigation areas, but it does not establish causation.

The current analysis does not include:

- Customer IDs
- Historical inventory levels
- Stockout dates
- Promotion history
- Campaign data
- Actual discount history
- Website or store traffic
- Competitor pricing
- Product cost
- Product margin

Additional data would be required for a stronger root-cause analysis.
