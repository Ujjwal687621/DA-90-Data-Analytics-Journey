# Executive Insights

**Project:** Ujjsha Retail Executive Dashboard

## Revenue Trend Overview

Revenue performance showed a strong upward trajectory from May to June before moderating across July and August.

### Monthly Revenue

| Month  | Revenue |
| ------ | ------: |
| May    |   ~$40K |
| June   |   ~$85K |
| July   |   ~$57K |
| August |   ~$43K |

## Month-over-Month Performance

| KPI                    |   Value |
| ---------------------- | ------: |
| Previous Month Revenue | $40.33K |
| MoM Growth             | $44.43K |
| MoM Growth %           | 110.16% |

## Executive Summary

The business more than doubled revenue between May and June, demonstrating significant short-term growth before revenue moderated in later months.

This trend highlights the importance of monitoring monthly performance rather than relying solely on cumulative revenue.
