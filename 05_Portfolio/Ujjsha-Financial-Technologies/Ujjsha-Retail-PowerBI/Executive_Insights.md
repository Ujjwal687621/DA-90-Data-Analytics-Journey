# Executive Insights

**Project:** Ujjsha Retail — Executive Sales Performance & Revenue Driver Analysis

---

## Revenue Trend Overview

Revenue increased sharply from May to June before moderating across July and August.

### Monthly Revenue

| Month  | Revenue |
| ------ | ------: |
| May    | $40.33K |
| June   | $84.75K |
| July   | $57.24K |
| August | $42.55K |

June was the highest-revenue month at **$84.75K**.

Revenue subsequently declined to **$57.24K** in July and **$42.55K** in August.

---

## Current Month Performance

The Executive Trends page uses dynamic time-intelligence measures that respond to the selected month.

When no month is selected, the report defaults to the latest available month, August.

| KPI                    | August Value |
| ---------------------- | -----------: |
| Current Month Revenue  |      $42.55K |
| Previous Month Revenue |      $57.24K |
| MoM Growth ($)         |     -$14.69K |
| MoM Growth (%)         |      -25.66% |

This indicates that August revenue declined by **$14.69K**, or **25.66%**, compared with July.

---

## Month Selection Behavior

The Executive Trends page was designed to remain interactive when users select a specific month.

For example:

- No month selected → August is used as the current month.
- June selected → June becomes the current month and July is not used as the comparison month.
- August selected → August becomes the current month and July becomes the comparison month.

This allows management to evaluate month-over-month performance within the selected time context.

## Executive Insight

> Revenue peaked at $84.75K in June after increasing 110.16% from May, then declined to $57.24K in July and $42.55K in August. The trend indicates strong mid-period growth followed by moderation in the subsequent two months.

---

## Business Interpretation

The revenue trend shows that the strongest period occurred in June, followed by two consecutive monthly declines.

The August decline is particularly important because it occurred alongside a broader reduction in transaction and unit volume.

Further investigation is therefore required to determine whether the decline was related to:

- Product availability
- Product mix
- Pricing
- Promotions or discounts
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Competitive conditions

The available dataset identifies the performance change but does not establish the underlying cause.
