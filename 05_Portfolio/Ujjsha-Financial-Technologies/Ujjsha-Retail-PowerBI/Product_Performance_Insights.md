# Ujjsha Retail — Product Performance Insights

## Overview

The Product Performance analysis evaluates revenue contribution across the Ujjsha Retail product portfolio and identifies concentration among products and categories.

The analysis was completed in Power BI as part of Portfolio Project #1.

---

## Revenue Concentration

## Executive Interpretation

The analysis shows that revenue is concentrated among a relatively small group of products and categories. The Top 5 products account for 51.62% of total revenue, while the Computer and Networking categories account for 68.98% of total revenue.

The Mesh Wifi System is the largest individual revenue contributor, while the USB-C Cable is the smallest.

---

## Management Investigation

Management should prioritize investigating revenue concentration in a small group of products and categories to understand the factors driving this concentration and assess potential risks from reliance on a limited product mix.

Potential areas for further investigation include:

- Product pricing
- Unexpected price changes
- Promotions and discounts
- Product availability
- Customer demand
- Inventory levels
- Stockout history

These areas require additional data before causal conclusions can be made.

---

## Data Limitations

The current dataset does not include:

- Historical inventory levels
- Stockout dates
- Historical pricing changes
- Promotion or discount history
- Product cost or gross margin
- Customer-level purchase information

Therefore, the analysis identifies revenue patterns and concentration but does not establish the underlying causes of those patterns.
