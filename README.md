## Portfolio Project #1 — Ujjsha Retail

**Focus:** Sales Performance & Revenue Driver Analysis

**Primary Tools:** SQL, Excel, Power BI, DAX

Portfolio Project #1 analyzes fictional retail sales data to understand revenue performance, product concentration, monthly trends, and the primary drivers of a month-over-month revenue decline.

The project follows an end-to-end analytical workflow:

**Business Problem → Data → Cleaning → Analysis → Modeling → Visualization → Business Findings → Management Investigation**

### Key Findings

- Total revenue: **$224.87K**
- Total transactions: **200**
- Total units sold: **742**
- Average selling price: **$303.06**
- Average items per transaction: **3.71**
- Top 5 products generated **51.62%** of total revenue
- Computer and Networking products generated **68.98%** of total revenue
- Revenue peaked at **$84.75K in June**
- August revenue declined to **$42.55K**, a **25.66% decrease** from July
- Computer and Networking products accounted for **90.44% of the August net revenue decline**

The analysis identifies where revenue changed and highlights areas requiring additional business investigation. The available dataset does not include information such as inventory levels, promotions, customer-level behavior, competitor activity, costs, or margins, so causal conclusions are intentionally limited.

## Analytics Workflow

The projects in this repository follow a practical analytics workflow:

**1. Define the Business Problem**

Understand the business question and identify the decisions the analysis should support.

**2. Prepare the Data**

Inspect, clean, validate, and structure the available data.

**3. Analyze**

Use SQL, Excel, Power BI, and other analytical tools to identify trends, patterns, relationships, and performance changes.

**4. Model**

Build appropriate data models, calculations, KPIs, and analytical structures.

**5. Visualize**

Present findings through clear reports and interactive dashboards.

**6. Investigate**

Identify potential drivers, limitations, and questions requiring additional business or operational data.

**7. Communicate**

Translate analytical results into clear business findings and recommendations.

## Data & Project Philosophy

Projects in this repository use fictional or practice datasets unless otherwise stated.

The objective is not simply to demonstrate that a tool can produce a result. Each project emphasizes:

- Business context
- Analytical reasoning
- Data validation
- Clear documentation
- Appropriate interpretation
- Honest treatment of limitations
- Actionable business questions

Where the available data cannot establish causation, the analysis distinguishes between observed patterns and hypotheses requiring further investigation.
