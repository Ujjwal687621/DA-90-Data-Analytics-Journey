### 5. Business Analysis Review

Reviewed the project's analytical conclusions to ensure that findings were separated from assumptions about causation.

The analysis identifies where revenue declined and which categories and products contributed to the decline, while recognizing that the available dataset does not establish the underlying cause.

The project therefore identifies additional areas for management investigation, including:

- Inventory availability
- Stockouts
- Pricing changes
- Promotions and discounts
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Product mix
- Competitive pricing
- Product demand

The project also identifies competitive pricing analysis as a potential next investigation for the Mesh Wi-Fi System while recognizing the importance of evaluating profitability and margin.

## Key Learning

The most important lesson from the finalization process was that a strong analytics portfolio project should not simply demonstrate technical tools.

It should demonstrate the ability to:

**Start with a business question → analyze the data → identify meaningful findings → recognize limitations → avoid unsupported conclusions → recommend a practical next investigation → communicate the result clearly.**

Project #1 now demonstrates this complete workflow.
